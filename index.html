01 // Background

About

I am a Salesforce Marketing Cloud Developer and Data Analyst based in Mumbai, with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow. Working at BrinkView LLP, my engineering focuses on the intersection of data architecture, lifecycle automation, and campaign analytics across Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Sales Cloud, and Data Cloud.

On the platform development side, I design and maintain normalized Data Extensions, write complex SQL queries for audience segmentation, and build multi-step journeys in Journey Builder and Automation Studio. On the analytics side, I specialize in connecting disparate CRM datasets, auditing data hygiene, and tracking post-deployment funnel performance to evaluate engagement trends and attribution.

With four Salesforce certifications—including Data360 Consultant and Agentforce Specialist—I focus on scaling marketing operations into Marketing Cloud Next and Data Cloud data graphs, replacing repetitive manual processes with resilient automation, and documenting what I build.
